# OnePlus 9 Darwin 27.2 status

The [public status page](../docs/STATUS.md) records the OnePlus 9 LE2110
hardware results for 24B119. The joint USB RAM boot reached SpringBoard
with A660 sysmem Metal submissions, S6SY792 touch initialized, press and release events from all three
PMIC keys, and valid BQ28Z610 gauge readings.

The hardware evidence covers a short boot. Gesture coverage, key-driven UI behavior and charger transitions require
device-side acceptance. The longer
A660 interaction runs and the software control are summarized in the
[known limitations](../docs/LIMITATIONS.md).

Follow the [reproduction guide](../docs/REPRODUCE.md) for source builds,
device-matched inputs and the offline package.
